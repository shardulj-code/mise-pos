# 💼 MISE — Restaurant Solution Proposal & Client Pitch

## Executive Summary
**Mise** is a high-speed, local-first restaurant operating system designed to eliminate the exorbitant recurring subscriptions and complex lock-ins imposed by legacy software providers like Petpooja, CaptainPad, and POSist.

With Mise, restaurants get an enterprise-grade Kitchen Display System (KDS), Waiter Ordering, Counter Billing with statutory GST compliance, Grocery/Pantry audit, and 24/7 self-service diagnostic support—without paying monthly or annual subscription fees.

## 🌟 The 5 Key Modules Included

### 1. 📝 Waiter Module (Handheld Ordering)
- Grid layout with color-coded table occupancy (Free vs. Occupied).
- Real-time search (`Ctrl+K` or type to filter dishes).
- One-tap item additions with cooking instructions (e.g. "Less spicy").
- Instant "Send KOT to Kitchen" transmission.

### 2. 🍳 Kitchen Display System (KDS & KOT)
- Dark-mode, high-contrast display designed for hot, busy kitchens.
- Live order status columns: **New Orders**, **Cooking**, and **Ready for Pickup**.
- Color-coded urgency timers (flashes amber when order exceeds 15 minutes).
- **86 / Out-of-Stock Button:** Instantly flags an exhausted ingredient or dish, preventing waiters from taking orders for unavailable items.

### 3. 💳 Counter & Billing Terminal
- Complete integer-paise Indian GST calculations (automatic 2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST composite split).
- Flexible tender modes: Cash (with auto change calculator), UPI QR (scan to pay), and Debit/Credit Cards.
- Clean 80mm and 58mm thermal receipt printing.

### 4. 📊 Manager Control Room & Pantry Audit
- Real-time gross sales, tax collected, and net revenue counters.
- **Pantry / Grocery Audit:** Track opening stock, purchases, usage, and shrinkage for oil, paneer, chicken, spices, and veggies.
- **Menu Editor:** Add new dishes, change prices, update GST rates on the fly.
- **Thermal Printer Manager:** Enter IP address and port (9100) with reachability check and test slips.
- **Day Close (Z-Report):** Generates day-end reconciliation slip and locks transactions.

### 5. 🤖 24/7 "Ask Mise" Self-Service Chatbot
- Embedded AI troubleshooter that understands Hinglish and restaurant terminology.
- One-tap quick diagnostics for common issues:
  - *“Thermal printer not printing KOT”*
  - *“How do I update GST tax slab?”*
  - *“Kitchen device disconnected”*
  - *“End-of-day settlement / Day Close”*
- Replaces costly call-center support contracts with instant automated fixes.
